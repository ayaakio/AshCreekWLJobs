Preamble

Territorial Penal Code

"The peace and prosperity of the Territory depend upon the faithful enforcement of its laws."

The Territorial Penal Code is established for the preservation of peace, the protection of property and the administration of justice throughout the Territory of Ash Creek. Every Deputy shall faithfully enforce these statutes with fairness, restraint and impartiality, whilst every citizen shall remain equally subject to the laws herein.

Title I

Crimes Against Public Order

§101 Disorderly Conduct

Any person who unlawfully disturbs the peace through disorderly, violent or offensive behaviour commits the offence of Disorderly Conduct.

Imprisonment 2 Minutes
Fine $5
