Preamble

Purpose and Authority

The Territorial Penal Code is established for the preservation of peace, the protection of property and the administration of justice throughout the Territory of Ash Creek.

Every resident, traveller and visitor within the Territory shall remain equally subject to the laws contained herein. Every Deputy of the Ash Creek Sheriff’s Office shall enforce these statutes with fairness, restraint and impartiality.

No provision of this Code shall be used to excuse cruelty, corruption or the misuse of lawful authority.

Title VII

General Provisions

700 Series

General Provision

§701 Attempt

A person who clearly intends to commit an offence and takes a direct act toward its completion may be charged with attempting that offence, notwithstanding that the offence was not completed.

General Provision

§702 Accomplice Liability

A person who knowingly assists, encourages, transports, supplies or protects another in the commission of an offence may be charged as an accomplice.

General Provision

§703 Multiple Offences

Where several distinct offences arise from the same incident, each offence may be charged separately where supported by the facts.

General Provision

§704 Combined Sentences

A combined sentence arising from one incident should ordinarily not exceed 45 minutes. A longer sentence may be imposed where authorised by a Judge, the Sheriff or a formal territorial court proceeding.

A 45-minute sentence for Murder may be imposed in full without being reduced by this provision.

General Provision

§705 Deputy Discretion

A Deputy may issue a warning or reduce a punishment for a minor offence where the accused is cooperative and no person has suffered serious harm. Such discretion shall not be exercised through favouritism or corruption.

General Provision

§706 Judicial Authority

A territorial Judge retains authority to interpret this Code, resolve disputes of law and impose an appropriate lawful judgment where the circumstances require formal proceedings.
