Preamble

Territorial Penal Code

"The peace and prosperity of the Territory depend upon the faithful enforcement of its laws."

The Territorial Penal Code is established for the preservation of peace, the protection of property, and the administration of justice throughout the Territory of Ash Creek. Every Deputy of the Sheriff's Office shall faithfully enforce these statutes with fairness, restraint and impartiality. Every citizen, resident and visitor shall remain equally subject to the laws contained within this Code.

Title I

Crimes Against Public Order

This Title establishes offences relating to public peace, lawful authority and the orderly administration of the Territory.

The individual statutes contained within this Title shall appear here.

Title II

Crimes Against Property

This Title establishes offences involving theft, burglary, criminal damage, livestock, horses and stolen property.

Title III

Crimes Against Persons

This Title establishes offences involving violence against another person, including assault, attempted murder and murder.

Title IV

Crimes Against Government

This Title establishes offences against the lawful authority of the Territorial Government and Sheriff's Office.

Title V

Weapons Offences

This Title establishes offences concerning the unlawful carrying, display and discharge of weapons.

Title VI

Crimes Against Commerce

This Title establishes offences relating to robbery of banks, trains, stagecoaches, merchants and travellers.

General

General Provisions

Authority

Deputies shall enforce the provisions of this Code impartially and without prejudice.

Multiple Offences

Where a person commits more than one offence during a single incident, Deputies may apply multiple charges where appropriate.

Judicial Authority

Judges of the Territory retain the authority to interpret this Code and impose lawful judgments where circumstances require.
